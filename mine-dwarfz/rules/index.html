The gameRules

Mine Dwarfz

Rules

No fairy tales, no garden gnomes. This one plays dirty.

Welcome to the Mine

Welcome to the mine of Mount Greed! Forget the little dwarves of fairy tales: here the dwarves are greedy, treacherous and ready for anything to pile up gems.

Stealing, betraying, sabotaging, even bringing the mine down on their own kin: anything goes to become the richest dwarf still alive on Mount Greed!

Goal of the Game

Collect precious gems before the other players. But watch out: the mine is unstable and could collapse at any moment!

You win if you:

  • hold 4 Gems, or
  • complete a Mission, or
  • escape the collapse of the mine with the fewest gems and cards.
The game lasts 10–20 minutes: play fast, play dirty, and keep an eye on the Collapse Counter.

Cards

  • Play: actions for your turn.
  • Fast Play: actions you can play at any time.
  • Gem: gems with no ability.
  • Magic Gem: gems with a special ability.
  • Calamity: events that push the mine toward collapse.
  • Mission: other ways to win.
  • Mountain: lasting effects for the player who plays them.
  • Special Card: the Repairman.
  • Collapse Counter: levels 0, 1, 2, 3 and Mine Collapsed!!

Setup

  1. Take all Play and Fast Play cards, shuffle them and deal 5 to each player.
  2. Shuffle the rest of them with all the other cards: this is the draw deck.
  3. Put the Collapse Counter in the middle of the table, at level 0.
  4. Choose the first player at random.

Your Turn

Play goes clockwise. Each turn has three phases.

1 · Draw (mandatory)

Draw a card from the deck. You cannot refuse. Only the first player skips this draw, on their very first turn.

Calamity! Reveal it at once, raise the Collapse Counter by 1 and discard it: it never goes into your hand.
2 · Action

Take as many actions as you like, each time playing one of these cards from your hand:

  • Play: resolve its effect, then discard it.
  • Mission: place it face up in front of you. It stays for the whole game: an extra way to win.
  • Mountain: place it face up in front of you. It stays for the whole game; its effect works only for you.

Gems (normal and magic) are never played as an action: they stay in your hand and count toward victory.

Empty Deck

Shuffle the discard pile to make a new deck. Missions and Mountains on the table stay there; those in the discard pile are shuffled in.

3 · Bonus Draw (optional)

If you took at least one action this turn (even a neutralized Play), you may draw one more card at the end of your turn. However many actions you take, you never draw more than one card this way.

Hand limit of 7: at the end of your turn, if you hold more than 7 cards, discard down to 7.

Fast Plays

Play them at any time, even on another player’s turn, in response to another card. Then discard them. Perfect for defending yourself or wrecking other players’ plans.

Target

On Play cards, the word in italics is the target. Fast Plays such as Pass the Buck can change it.

Magic Gems

Discard a Magic Gem from your hand to use its power. The card tells you when: during your turn, or at any time.

Collapse Counter

It shows how unstable the mine is. It starts at 0 and goes up and down because of the cards. When it reaches Mine Collapsed!! the mine collapses at once and the game ends.

End of the Game

The game ends immediately when:

1 · Riches

A player holds 4 or more Gems (normal or magic): that player wins!

2 · Mission Accomplished

A player meets the condition of their Mission in play (e.g. the Collector needs 3 specific Gems): that player wins!

3 · The Mine Collapses
  • If a player has Mission: Saboteur in play and the mine collapses during their turn, that player wins.
  • Otherwise the player with the fewest Gems in hand wins.
  • Tie: fewest cards in hand wins.
  • Still a tie: the player who caused the collapse wins; if they are not among the tied players, the tied player closest clockwise to them wins.

Neutralize: cancel the effect of a Play (the card is discarded anyway).
